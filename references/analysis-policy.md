# Derived analysis policy

Similar-task lookup is deliberately conservative: normalized `task_type` is the match basis, and ordered actor/event types form a path signature. Results are candidates for human analysis, not a semantic identity claim.

A future `BEST_KNOWN_PATH` claim must include source task/path IDs, method version, sample size, observation window, confidence and explicit treatment of adoption, quality, corrections, failures, recovery, human effort, duration, cost, risk and prerequisites. It must be supersedable and must never erase historical alternatives.

Do not create Skill, automation or Agent implementations automatically. Produce reviewable candidates with lineage and seek separate authorization.
